Qalam — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 August 2026 · App: Qalam (com.technion.qalam) · A student project of Technion course 236272

Qalam helps people learn to write Arabic letters by hand. We collect the minimum data the app needs, keep it private by default, and never sell it or use it for advertising.

Accounts

The account holder signs in once (email/password or Google Sign-In) to set the app up. The account collects the account holder's email address only. A settings area is additionally protected by a user-created PIN.

What we collect

  • Learner profile: a nickname entered at setup and practice progress (which letters were practiced, repetitions, mastery). Stored on the device and in our cloud database (Google Firebase) under the anonymous or account identity.
  • Account: the email address, used only for sign-in and account management.
  • Handwriting: strokes are recognized and scored on the device. Raw handwriting and drawings are never uploaded and never stored on our servers.
  • AI coaching summaries: to generate the tutor's feedback, the app sends a limited practice summary (letter, exercise type, and scoring outcome) to our tutor service on Google Cloud, processed with Google Vertex AI. This summary contains no names, no free text, and no handwriting images — the allowed fields are a fixed technical whitelist verified by automated tests.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties for marketing.
  • No location collection, no contacts, no camera or microphone access.
  • No social features, no public profiles, no messaging.

Where data lives

Cloud data is stored with Google Firebase (Firestore) and processed on Google Cloud. Practice data is keyed to an anonymous identity unless an account is linked. Access is restricted by security rules.

Data retention & deletion

Local data can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of cloud data (the profile, progress, or an account), email us at the address below and we will delete it.
